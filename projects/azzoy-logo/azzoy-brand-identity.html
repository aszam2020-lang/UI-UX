02   The Symbol

The Gate

A geometric construction from two converging strokes and a single crossbar. The form reads as an architectural gateway — monumental in proportion, precise in execution. The apex is a point. There is nothing unnecessary in it.

Canvas 4 : 5 ratio — 80 × 100 units
Stroke weight 2.6 pt at reference size
Line termination Square cap — architectural, definite
Crossbar position 40.5% from apex — classical A proportion
Apex join Miter, limit 20 — sharp, precise

The Double Z
A ZZ OY

The consecutive Z's are not a challenge to be solved — they are the visual signature of the house. In Cormorant Garamond, the Z carries a fine diagonal that creates inherent movement and tension. Two together produce a visual energy that the rest of the word — the open O and the branching Y — resolves.

Colour philosophy

The palette contains no gold. Gold is the shorthand for luxury that serious luxury does not need. The warmth is in the white — a bone that has been in the Egyptian sun. The black is not cold — it carries a trace of warm undertone. These are not neutral colours. They are considered ones.

08   System Principles
The mark does not explain itself

There is no Egypt in the symbol. There is no cotton in the colour. There is no founder in the monogram. The identity is a construction of proportion and restraint — it earns its associations rather than announcing them.

Wordmark takes precedence

In the hierarchy of the identity system, the wordmark is primary. The symbol is a house mark — for garment labels, packaging seals, embossing. It supports the word. It does not replace it.

No gold. No gradients. No effects

The identity is two colours. It does not require metallics, embossing effects in digital contexts, or texture to communicate quality. Those come in the physical materials — the paper, the cloth, the box — not in the mark.

Space is the luxury

The wordmark requires generous clear space on all sides — a minimum of the cap height of the wordmark on every edge. In practice, more. Crowding the mark is the first sign that something has gone wrong.
